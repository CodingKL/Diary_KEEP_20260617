COMPLETING THE COMPREHENSIVE FINAL (CERTIFICATION) EXAM

Per state regulations, you will be eligible to take the COMPREHENSIVE FINAL EXAM after completing all of the chapter content and instructional reviews. You are not required to pass the comprehensive final exam. However, we strongly recommend passing the comprehensive final exam with a score of at least 70%.

Your course completion certificate will be available to download and print upon successful completion of the following:

All pre-licensing content and instructional reviews,
The COMPREHENSIVE FINAL EXAM,
12 hours of study time, and
The post-course STUDENT ATTESTATION.
