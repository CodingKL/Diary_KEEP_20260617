
BE ABLE TO IDENTIFY:
THE CALIFORNIA INSURANCE CODE AND HOW IT MAY BE CHANGED;

The California Insurance Code consists of statutes established by the state legislature and signed into law by the Governor of California. There are five divisions in the CIC: General Rules Governing Insurance, Classes of Insurance, The Insurance Commissioner, Affordable Housing Entities Risk Retention Pool, and Insurance Adjusters. The California Insurance Code is updated by the legislature passing new statutes to add to, amend, or repeal existing statues.

THE CALIFORNIA CODE OF REGULATIONS (CCR TITLE 10, CHAPTER 5) AND HOW IT MAY BE CHANGED; AND,

The California Code of Regulations is necessary to administer the California Insurance Code. While the CIC is written and passed by the executive and legislative branches of the California state government, the CCR consists of rules issued by the California Insurance Commissioner. The California code of regulations is used by the commissioner to enforce the CIC and may be changed or withdrawn by the commissioner as he deems necessary. While it is true that the California Code of Regulations are not technically laws, they carry the same weight as law and any person who violates a regulation is subject to the same penalty as someone who violates the code.

HOW THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER IS SELECTED AND THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE POSITION, SECTIONS 12900 AND 12921 OF THE CIC.

The Commissioner of insurance oversees the insurance industry in California. This is an elected position that is held for a term of 4 years with a two-term limit. The election of commissioner takes place during the same general election as the governor. If the position of insurance commissioner becomes vacant, the governor may appoint a replacement with the approval of the legislature. The commissioner shall perform all duties imposed upon him or her by the provisions of the code and other laws regulating the business of insurance in the state, and shall enforce the execution of those provisions and laws. In general, the Commissioner's duties and powers include:

• Revokes and suspends licenses for just cause
• Licenses, regulates, and examines insurance companies
• Enforce state insurance laws and regulations
• Investigate complaints regarding the insurance industry
• Provide consumer education
• Establish fees for services
• Adopt emergency regulations if necessary
• Report annually to the government
• Rate regulation
• Consumer protection
• Insurance company solvency surveillance
