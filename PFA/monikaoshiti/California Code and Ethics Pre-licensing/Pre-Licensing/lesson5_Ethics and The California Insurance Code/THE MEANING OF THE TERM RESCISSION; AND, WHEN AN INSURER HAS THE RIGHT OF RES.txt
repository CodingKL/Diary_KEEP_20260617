THE MEANING OF THE TERM RESCISSION; AND, WHEN AN INSURER HAS THE RIGHT OF RESCISSION;

Rescission is the overturning or unmaking of a contract between parties. In other words, this involves the cancellation of a contract. The cancellation will go back retroactively to the inception date of the contract as though the contract never existed.
INTENTIONAL AND FRAUDULENT OMISSION, SECTION 338 OF THE CIC
An intentional and fraudulent omission, on the part of one insured, to communicate information of matters proving or tending to prove the falsity of a warranty, entitles the insurer to rescind.
FALSE MATERIAL REPRESENTATION, SECTION 359 OF THE CIC
If a representation is false in a material point, whether affirmative or promissory, the injured party is entitled to rescind the contract from the time the representation becomes false.
VIOLATION OF A MATERIAL WARRANTY OR PROVISION, SECTION 447 OF THE CIC
The violation of a material warranty or other material provision of a policy, on the part of either party thereto, entitles the other to rescind.
CONCEALMENT, SECTION 331 OF THE CIC;
Know that either intentional or unintentional concealment entitles an injured party to rescission of a contract.
