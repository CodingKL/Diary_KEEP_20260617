BE ABLE TO IDENTIFY AND APPLY THE MEANING OF THE FOLLOWING:
PLACE THE CUSTOMER'S INTEREST FIRST;

The basic objective of any code of ethics is to serve the public interest and place the customer’s interest first by specifying and enforcing the minimum ethical conduct expected of an insurance producer and its principal (i.e., insurer). In addition, another ultimate goal of any code of conduct is to prevent unethical conduct and unfair practices. According to Section 791 of State law, the California Insurance Code and the California Code of Regulations specify numerous illegal insurance practices and impose sanctions or penalties on those who violate insurance law.

KNOW YOUR JOB - AND CONTINUE TO INCREASE YOUR LEVEL OF COMPETENCE;
It is the responsibility of any licensed insurance producer to increase his or her level of competence and professionalism by completing continuing education courses throughout their career. The State of California has in place continuing education requirements to ensure that licensees maintain competence and trustworthiness.

IDENTIFY THE CUSTOMER’S NEEDS AND RECOMMEND PRODUCTS AND SERVICES THAT MEET THOSE NEEDS;
The producer’s interests and biases should not be a factor in any insurance transaction. Producers should remain independent and objective in their evaluations and recommend insurance products that will meet the needs of and benefit the policyowner.

ACCURATELY AND TRUTHFULLY REPRESENT PRODUCTS AND SERVICES;

The producer must be committed to providing a prospective purchaser an honest explanation of the policies and services he or she offers. Generally, a producer will base sales activity on the assumption that insurance solves problems and reduces uncertainty. Therefore, producers assist their clients in identifying insurance needs and demonstrating how such needs can be satisfied utilizing insurance products.

AVOID JARGON; USE LAYPERSON’S LANGUAGE WHEN POSSIBLE;

When a licensee is interviewing a customer, he or she must use simple and understandable language (i.e., laymen’s language). Industry terms and concepts can sometimes sound confusing to those who are not themselves producers, using simple language is important in making sure the customer understands all the products and services that are offered.

STAY IN TOUCH WITH CUSTOMERS AND CONDUCT PERIODIC COVERAGE REVIEWS;

Relationships are built between the client and the producer when the latter determines a potential client’s insurance needs, recommends a product or solution and develops a long-term relationship following the sale. This relationship includes maintaining contact with the insured and conducting periodic coverage reviews to ensure that the clients insurance needs are still being met.

MAINTAIN CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROTECT THE PRIVACY OF CUSTOMER INFORMATION USING PHYSICAL AND ELECTRONIC SAFEGUARDS

The California Code establishes standards for the collection, use and disclosure of information gathered in the process of engaging in insurance transactions. The purpose of these standards is to maintain a balance between the need for information by those conducting the business of insurance and the public’s demand for privacy. A producer is responsible for protecting this confidential relationship with all of their clients.

KEEP INFORMED OF AND OBEY ALL INSURANCE LAWS AND REGULATIONS;

From time to time the laws and regulations pertaining to insurance can be changed by the legislature. It is the responsibility of the producer to remain up to date on these changes and follow all insurance laws and regulations.

PROVIDE EXEMPLARY SERVICE TO YOUR CLIENTS; AND,

It is the responsibility of all producers to place the needs of the customer first and provide exemplary service to all customers while doing so.

AVOID UNFAIR OR INACCURATE REMARKS ABOUT THE COMPETITION.

While describing their own products and services, a producer must also avoid providing unfair or inaccurate remarks about their competition. A producer must always remain truthful when interacting with a client.
