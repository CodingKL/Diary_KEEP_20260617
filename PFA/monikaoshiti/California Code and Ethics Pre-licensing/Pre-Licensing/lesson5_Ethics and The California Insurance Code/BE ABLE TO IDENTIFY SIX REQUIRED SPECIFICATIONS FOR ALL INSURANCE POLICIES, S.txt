BE ABLE TO IDENTIFY SIX REQUIRED SPECIFICATIONS FOR ALL INSURANCE POLICIES, SECTION 381 OF THE CIC.

Generally, insurance contracts include six specifications. In other words, a insurance policy must specify or identify the following items:
• the parties between whom the contract is made;
• the property or life to be insured;
• the insurable interest of the contract owner if he or she is not the insured or absolute owner;
• any risks insured against (i.e., life, disability, medical expenses, damage, etc.);
• the period during which the insurance is to continue; and
• either a statement of the premium, or if the insurance is of a character where the exact premium is only determinable upon the termination of the contract, a statement of the basis and rates upon which the final premium is to be determined and paid.
