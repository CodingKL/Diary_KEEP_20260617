AGENCY NAME, FICTITIOUS NAME, USE OF NAME [CIC SECTIONS 1724.5 AND 1729.5]

Section 1724.5 of the CIC states that every individual and organization licensee and every applicant for such a license shall file with the Commissioner in writing the true name of the individual or organization and also all fictitious names under which he or she conducts or intends to conduct his or her business and after licensing shall file with the Commissioner any change in or discontinuance of such names. Such fictitious names may be disapproved in writing by the Commissioner if he or she feels that the name is: too similar to a name already used by another licensee; the use of the name may mislead the public; the name implies that the licensee is an underwriter (this does not include CLU or Chartered Life Underwriter if the licensee holds this life insurance designation); or the licensee has already filed and not discontinued the use of more than two names including the true name. The licensee may not use the fictitious name after being notified by the Commissioner in writing that such use is contrary to this section of the CIC.
Section 1729.5 of the CIC explains that a property broker-agent, casualty broker-agent, or life agent who has a service contract with or who is a stockholder or member of any incorporated association or corporation licensed under the CIC and organized under the Corporations Code for the purpose of providing services to property broker-agents, casualty broker-agents, or life agents may use the name of that corporation or association on any stationery or advertisements and other written or printed matter provided that the name of the property broker-agent, casualty broker-agent, or life agent is clearly identified as bearing only that relationship to the corporation or association in one of the following ways:

“Representing ____;”
“A stockholder of ____;”
“Placing business through ____;”
“Using services of ____.”


The use of the corporation or association name in the manner provided in this section shall not constitute such use as would mislead the public within the meaning of Section 1724.5.


CHANGE OF ADDRESS [CIC SECTION 1729]

Section 1729 of the CIC states that every licensee and license applicant must notify the Commissioner immediately in writing of a change in his or her address as given on the application. Every licensed corporation must file a written notice with the Commissioner of all changes, except address changes, of its stockholders who own 10% or more of the corporation’s stock and of all officers and directors of the corporation.

INTERNET ADVERTISEMENTS [CIC SECTION 1726]

Section 1726 of the CIC states that a person who is licensed in this State as an insurance producer and who advertises insurance on the Internet shall identify all of the following information on the Internet, regardless of whether the insurance producer maintains his or her Internet presence or if the presence is maintained on his or her behalf:
• his or her name as it appears on the insurance license and any fictitious name approved by the Commissioner;
• the State of his or her residence (i.e., domicile) and principle place of business;
• his or her license number; and
• the word “insurance.”

It will be considered transacting insurance if a producer does any of the following:

• Solicits a California resident;
• Provides an insurance quote to a California resident;
• Accepts an application for coverage from a California resident;
• Communicates with a California resident regarding one or more terms of an agreement to provide insurance or an insurance policy;
• Enters into negotiations before executing an insurance contract with a California resident; or
• Transacts matters after executing an insurance contract with a California resident that arises out of that contract.
