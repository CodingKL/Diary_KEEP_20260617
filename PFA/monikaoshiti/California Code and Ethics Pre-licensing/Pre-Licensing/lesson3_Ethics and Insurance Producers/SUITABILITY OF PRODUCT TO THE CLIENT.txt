SUITABILITY OF PRODUCT TO THE CLIENT

Suitability of product to the client means that the producer and insurer must make sure that the purchase of certain insurance policies are suitable for the purchaser. Publications such as a Buyer’s Guide will assist the consumer in determining the appropriateness or suitability of the recommended purchase or replacement in order to prevent the sale of excessive insurance. Whenever a policy is surrendered and replaced with another, the consumer may be relinquishing benefits that have accrued over the years. Recommendations made by the producer are based upon information provided by the applicant or proposed insured at the time of application involving the applicant’s needs and objectives.
