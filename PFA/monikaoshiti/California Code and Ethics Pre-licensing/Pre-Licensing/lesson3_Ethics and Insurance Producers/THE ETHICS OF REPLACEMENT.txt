THE ETHICS OF REPLACEMENT

The fact that replacement is not illegal places it squarely in the realm of ethical behavior. Ethical replacement of life insurance really brings to life some of the codes of ethics we discussed earlier The reality is the agent will receive a commission if he replaces the other company’s contract, but is it the right thing to do? There are two tests you might try. The first is the familiar “golden rule.” Would you, in fact, make this replacement yourself if you were both policyholder and agent? Secondly, if you replace a contract tonight, and all the facts of the transaction are published in tomorrow’s newspaper for all your friends, relatives, clients and competitors to see, will they condone your actions? If you can truthfully answer yes to this question, make the replacement. You are on ethically sound footing. Don’t forget to complete the paperwork (the compliance part), to keep the market conduct rug under your feet as well.
