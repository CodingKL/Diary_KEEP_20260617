ETHICAL CONDUCT

Ethics involves morality issues. In other words, it involves what is right and wrong as applied to activities in any area of life including the insurance business. Every State Insurance Department now requires the study of ethics as a condition of license issuance and renewal. The Commissioner ensures that all intermediaries satisfy ethics requirements. An ethical intermediary increases his or her stature as an insurance professional as well as his or her opportunity for success in the insurance business.

Ethics is driven by a Code of Ethics. An act committed by a person may be legal but it also may not be ethical. A personal ethics code of behavior helps the individual gain personal and professional satisfaction. A practical code of ethics, governed by State laws, assists the individual in performing his or her fiduciary responsibilities and other responsibilities as an intermediary. This will also increase the productivity of the intermediary and avoid conflicts of interest as well. All codes of ethics are based upon the Golden Rule. This age old Rule of “do unto others as you would have them do unto you” is the foundation from which all “codes” are derived. All professional insurance associations and designation programs possess their own Code of Ethics.
