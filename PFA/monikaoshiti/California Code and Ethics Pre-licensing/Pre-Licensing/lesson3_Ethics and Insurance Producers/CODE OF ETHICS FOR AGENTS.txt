CODE OF ETHICS FOR AGENTS

One source of guidance for agents can be found in the various codes of ethics for agent and industry organizations. The American College Code of Ethics, for example, includes the Professional Pledge:
“In all my professional relationships, I pledge myself to the following rules of ethical conduct: I shall, in light of all conditions surrounding those I serve, which I shall make every effort to ascertain and understand, render that service which, in the same circumstances, I would apply to myself.”
The Million Dollar Round Table Code of Ethics reminds members and provisional applicants to:
“Always place the best interests of their clients above their own direct or indirect interests.”
Guide 1.1 of the Society of Financial Service Professionals Code of Ethics states:
“A member shall provide advice and service which are in the client’s best interest.”
The relationship of these industry codes of ethics to the proverbial golden rule is easy to recognize. So ethics for agents sounds a lot like what mom always said: “Be nice. Share with others. Don’t bite. If you don’t hit him, he won’t hit you.” We always knew mom was right, didn’t we?
