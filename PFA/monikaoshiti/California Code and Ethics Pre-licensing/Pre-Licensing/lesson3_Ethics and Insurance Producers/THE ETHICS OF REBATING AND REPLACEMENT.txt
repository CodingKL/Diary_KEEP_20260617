THE ETHICS OF REBATING AND REPLACEMENT

Every agent knows that in our industry “rebating” is a dirty word and “replacement” is less than lily white. Replacement of existing life insurance usually causes a ton of paperwork, and almost every State has a law against life insurance agents returning part of a premium to the insurance applicant as a rebate. But is replacement unethical? And, it’s fair to ask the question, “What’s wrong with rebating? Car companies and appliance stores do it all the time !”
