BASIC ETHICAL PRINCIPLES

The ethical process involves two steps:
(1) knowing what is right and what is wrong; and
(2) an individual must have the personal discipline, integrity and motivation to do what he or she knows is right.
State Insurance Departments have penalties in place to protect the public and to warn producers that illegal and unethical activities will not be tolerated. Determining the right course of action is a challenging process. In this imperfect system it is left to State regulators to determine the proper course of action. The Commissioner or Superintendent of Insurance, and his or her deputies, empowered with the responsibility of enforcing insurance laws that are on the books. Regulators will also follow specific principles to aid in determining what is a “right” or proper decision. Such principles may be adopted together as a group or applied individually. These are not listed in order of importance. This list is very subjective. When determining right from wrong these principles should be considered as a guide for. In some cases, these principles sometimes conflict with one another. However, such principles remain paramount.
