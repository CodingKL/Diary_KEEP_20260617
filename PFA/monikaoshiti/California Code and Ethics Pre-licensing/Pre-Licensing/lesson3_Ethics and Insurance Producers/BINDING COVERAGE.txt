BINDING COVERAGE

Insurance producers submit applications for coverage to the underwriting department of the insurer. When an application is completed and an initial premium is collected by the producer, a premium receipt is provided to the applicant. Some insurers utilize a binding receipt in some cases that binds coverage as of the date of the receipt. Also referred to as an unconditional receipt, issuance of a binding receipt means that coverage becomes effective as soon as the premium is collected by the producer (i.e., immediate coverage). The receipt will verify this since it allows for instant coverage. Insurance protection will continue during the underwriting period and remain in effect until, and if, the application is declined and premium is refunded. If the insured experiences a covered loss before this occurs, the insurer must pay the claim.
Most life insurers utilize a conditional receipt. This receipt indicates that the insurer will conditionally insure the proposed insured pending proof of insurability as long as an initial premium is paid. Therefore, coverage becomes effective as of the date of the conditional receipt (or if later, as of the date of the medical exam if the proposed insured is found to be insurable).
