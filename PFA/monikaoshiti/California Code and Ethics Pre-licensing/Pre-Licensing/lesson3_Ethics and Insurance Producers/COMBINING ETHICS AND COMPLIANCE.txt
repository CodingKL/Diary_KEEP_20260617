COMBINING ETHICS AND COMPLIANCE

Practicing good market conduct for the insurance sales professional means combining ethical conduct (the moral compass) with documentation (compliance) in all day-to-day business activities. Ethics is doing the right things. Compliance is documenting that you are doing the right things. For example, showing a prospect an NAIC approved illustration during the course of a sale is providing complete disclosure (doing the right thing), and having the applicant sign the illustration, as required by many State compliance rules, is the evidence that this was done (documentation of having done the right thing).
Combining ethics and compliance is also good for the future of the industry. To the extent that ethical agents and ethical companies influence their peers, they are contributing to perhaps preempting new compliance legislation in the future. The surest way to control the amount of new regulation of our industry is to eliminate the need for it.
