THE ETHICS OF REBATING

There is an interesting twist when you think about rebating and ethics. Rebating life insurance premiums is a case of a behavior being illegal but not necessarily unethical. Rebating part of the cost of a product to a consumer is not unethical per se. As noted earlier, car companies and many other retailers do it all the time — and customers love it! In fact, it’s gotten to the point where a person almost hates to buy a new car without a rebate! Of course, the argument can be made that if something is illegal, it is automatically unethical because it is unethical to break the law. Be that as it may, rebating life insurance premiums is illegal in most jurisdictions and should be avoided at all times and places regardless. The practice has been proven to deliver poor results for agents, companies, and consumers — new car rebates notwithstanding.
