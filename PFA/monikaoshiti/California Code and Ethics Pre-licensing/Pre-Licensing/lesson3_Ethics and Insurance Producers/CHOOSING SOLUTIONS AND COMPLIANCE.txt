CHOOSING SOLUTIONS AND COMPLIANCE

There is not a lot that can be said about compliance and choosing solutions. Choosing only products and companies that are licensed in your State should go without saying. Having all the proper licenses for the products you are going to propose should also go without saying. Most State insurance departments take a very dim view of agents who sell products without the proper authority.
Otherwise, insurance regulations have not yet developed to the point where they dictate what you must sell in any particular instance. However, a new concept may be on the horizon. Suitability is a word that is being used more and more often in insurance circles these days. Suitability is a concept that has long been a factor in selling mutual funds and other securities like variable life insurance and annuities. Sales of these products are required to meet strict suitability standards. Now, some insurers and State insurance departments are beginning to look at suitability requirements for other life insurance and annuity sales as well. The approach makes sense when you think about it. If a sale is suitable and it clearly meets the client’s needs, it’s more likely to stay on the books and less likely to develop into a disgruntled customer down the road.
If suitability standards for all insurance products become law, or if companies widely adopt suitability requirement on their own, agents will have another requirement to consider as they analyze cases to determine solutions. Of course, ethical insurance agents already consider suitability. They would want a solution to be suitable in their own situation and they want no less for the clients they serve.
