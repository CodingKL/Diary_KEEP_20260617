EVALUATING THE COURSE OF ACTION

Most ethical courses of action are not always clear and it may be useful to remember that ethical decision making is more of a process than an individual event. Any decision making process can be described as a series of steps. Like risk management, such steps can be summarized as: (1) identifying and defining the issues;
(2) gathering information and collecting the facts;
(3) identifying the available options available;
(4) deciding what options to utilize;
(5) determining a course of action;
(6) implementing the decision; and
(7) monitoring and making changes over time.
