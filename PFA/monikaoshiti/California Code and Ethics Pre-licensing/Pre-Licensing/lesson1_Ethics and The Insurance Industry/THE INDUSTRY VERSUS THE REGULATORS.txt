THE INDUSTRY VERSUS THE REGULATORS

Here again, there is a temptation to point a finger at the State and federal regulators and blame them for all the bad industry press. But the same rule described earlier still holds true. The Commissioners cannot make headlines when there is not industry abuse to attack. Recently, there seems to be an attitude change within much of the industry, which holds that the best way to look at the state and federal insurance regulators consuming public. After all, the regulators have either been elected to the position or appointed by other elected officials. In either case, they have been duly appointed via the democratic process to represent the consumer’s interest in matters of insurance; a product many consumers have difficulty understanding.
One of the more compelling bits of evidence of this new attitude is the recent formation by the industry of the Insurance Marketplace Standards Association (IMSA). The very fact that IMSA exists is testimony to the willingness of the industry to face ethics and market conduct issues head on by cooperating must more closely with industry customers and their designated representatives, the industry regulators both State and federal.
