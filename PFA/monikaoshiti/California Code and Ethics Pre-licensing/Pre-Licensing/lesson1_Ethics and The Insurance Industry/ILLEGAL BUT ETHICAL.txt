ILLEGAL, BUT ETHICAL

Consider the case of Mark, whose brother Joe was suffering from a painful illness. Joe and Mark were both poor, with families to support and no money to spare. The medication Joe needed to get well was expensive, and Joe had no health insurance. Mark did have insurance, and he asked his family physician to prescribe the medication in his name so that he could give it to Joe.
If you were the physician, what would you do in this situation? Prescribing medication falsely is clearly a violation of medical ethics and standards, as well as a violation of the law. But without it, Joe would suffer and perhaps die. What is the higher duty here, obeying the law or helping to relieve a human being’s suffering? There are no easy answers. Rules, standards, and laws protect us from liability and harm but do not always reconcile with the practical impulses that we face every day.
