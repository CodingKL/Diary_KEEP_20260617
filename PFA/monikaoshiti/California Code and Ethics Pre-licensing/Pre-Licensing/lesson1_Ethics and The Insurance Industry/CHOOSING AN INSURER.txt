CHOOSING AN INSURER

Choosing an insurance company to represent isn’t always a well informed decision. Individuals enter the insurance business with a particular company for a variety of reasons. It may be as simple as the fact that they need a job, and this one happens to be available. Many enter the business because they are recruited by someone whose description of the opportunities and rewards of the career sufficiently impressed them. The recruiter “sells” the company along with the position as any good employer representative would. In the end, the new recruit may have very little idea of the true nature of the company. As the agent matures in the position, however, he or she may become aware of company policies or conduct that warrants considering a move to a different company.
