INTRODUCTION


The dictionary will tell you the word ethics means something like “the systematized principles of morally correct behavior.” The word comes from the Greek ethos, which means character. An ethical person is someone with character. One who lives by principles and demonstrates morally correct behavior. Simply put, ethics is a set of moral principles or values that guides people’s behavior. Ethical behavior allows producers to earn the trust, respect, and loyalty of their clients. Insurance producers have an ethical responsibility to the general public, insurers, policy owners, and the state. This chapter will take a closer look at ethics as it relates to insurance and the impact ethical behavior can have on the insurance industry.
