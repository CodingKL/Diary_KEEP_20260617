
According to the California Insurance Code, 
which of the following is NOT an example of an insurable event?
A.An insured is admitted to the hospital for delivery of a newborn
B.An insured suffers a financial loss in the state lottery
C.A guest is injured by a fall from the insured’s driveway
D.An insured is sued for unintentional slander of another person

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
❌ An insured suffers a financial loss in the state lottery (or losing money in a poker game / gambling) is NOT an example of an insurable event under the California Insurance Code.

Explanation of the Correct Option
According to Section 250 of the California Insurance Code, an insurable event is defined as any contingent or unknown event, whether past or future, which may damnify a person having an insurable interest or create a liability against them.

Insurance can only protect against pure risks, which carry only the chance of a loss or no loss. A lottery or a poker game is a speculative risk, which involves a conscious choice to gamble for the chance of either a financial gain or a financial loss. Speculative risks are fundamentally uninsurable.
