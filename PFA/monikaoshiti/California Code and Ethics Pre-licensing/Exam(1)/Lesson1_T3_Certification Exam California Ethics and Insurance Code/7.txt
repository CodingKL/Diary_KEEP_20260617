

An expression of grievance against an insurer is called what?
A.A compliment
B.An issue
C.A complaint
D.An unfair trade practice

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An expression of grievance against an insurer is officially called a complaint or a grievance.In the insurance industry, these terms are often used interchangeably to describe any formal statement of dissatisfaction regarding the company's service, billing, claims handling, or the behavior of its agents.If you are dealing with a denied claim or believe your company has violated policy terms, you can file a formal complaint directly with your state’s regulatory body. You can verify your local regulations and file a report directly with the National Association of Insurance Commissioners, which will route you to your specific state's Department of Insurance.

How Do I File a Complaint Against My Insurance Company?
Do you have a complaint against your insurer? Has it treated you unfairly? Did you file a claim, but were unsatisfied with the result? In these situations, you can file a complaint against the company with your state department of insurance (DOI). Your DOI protects policyholders by sharing information about insurance and by making sure insurance companies follow the rules and treat consumers fairly.
