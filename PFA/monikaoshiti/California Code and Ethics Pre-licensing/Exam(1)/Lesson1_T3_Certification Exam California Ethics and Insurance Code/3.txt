
A representation may be altered or withdrawn
A.only before the insurance is in effect
B.anytime with the insurer’s approval
C.anytime with the applicant’s initials
D.only after the policy has been issued

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In insurance law, a representation can only be altered or withdrawn before the insurance contract goes into effect. Once the policy is issued and in effect, these statements cannot be changed. To review specific legal statutes governing contracts and policies, check the California Department of Insurance for state guidelines or refer to IRMI for standard industry definitions.
