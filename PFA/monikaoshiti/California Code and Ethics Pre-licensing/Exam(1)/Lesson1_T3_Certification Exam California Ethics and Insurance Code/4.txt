
A type of misrepresentation where an agent illegally induces a consumer to surrender a current policy of insurance in order to purchase a new one has engaged in:
A.Rebating
B.Twisting
C.Unfair discrimination
D.Defamation
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An agent who uses misrepresentation or incomplete comparison to illegally induce a consumer to surrender, lapse, or forfeit an existing insurance policy to buy a new one has engaged in twisting.
