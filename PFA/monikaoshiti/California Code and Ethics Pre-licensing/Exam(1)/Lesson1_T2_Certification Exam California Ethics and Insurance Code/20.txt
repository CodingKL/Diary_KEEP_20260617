

The purpose of securing an insurance license is 
to make sure that a licensee is competent and trustworthy. 
The Department of Insurance may deny an application 
for an insurance license for all of the following reasons, 
EXCEPT:

A.The license candidate has been convicted of a felony
B.The license candidate committed an act forbidden by the insurance code
C.The license candidate has been convicted of a misdemeanor
D.The license candidate aided another agent in an unfair practice
